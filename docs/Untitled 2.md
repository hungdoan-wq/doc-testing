The core purpose of this new dashboard is to provide **real-time operational awareness and immediate insights into ongoing testing activities and quality status.**

**Key User Questions it Answers:**

- What's happening _right now_ in our test executions?
    
- Are there any critical test failures occurring _at this moment_ that need immediate attention?
    
- What's the live progress of scheduled or ongoing test runs?
    
- What's the immediate health snapshot of our application based on the very latest test results?
    
- What were the results of tests that just finished (e.g., within the last hour or today so far)?
    

**Specific User Jobs-to-be-Done for the "Current" Dashboard:**

- **QA Engineers/Testers:**
    
    - Monitor the live progress and results of their own or team's active test executions.
        
    - Quickly identify tests that are currently failing or blocked.
        
- **QA Leads/Managers:**
    
    - Get a quick overview of the team's current testing load and any immediate critical issues (e.g., for daily huddles).
        
    - Monitor the stability of the testing environment in real-time.
        
    - Observe immediate quality indicators after a new build is deployed to a test environment.
        
- **Operations/DevOps (potentially):**
    
    - Check the health of the automated testing pipeline.
        

**Target Users for this Dashboard:**

- QA engineers actively running or monitoring tests.
    
- Automation engineers checking on live script executions.
    
- Operations teams or on-call personnel who need to understand current quality status.
    
- QA leads/managers wanting a quick glance at the immediate state of affairs without deep historical dives.
