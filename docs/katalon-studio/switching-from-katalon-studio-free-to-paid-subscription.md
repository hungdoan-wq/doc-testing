---
title: Switching from Katalon Studio Free to Paid Subscription
---


<p xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="shortdesc"> </p> 
<div xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="p">Yes, switching from the free to the paid version of Katalon Studio will not affect your tests or information.<ul className="ul"><li className="li">Katalon Studio is installed on your computer, and your test projects, scripts, and data are stored locally or in a connected repository like Git.</li><li className="li">Upgrading to a paid version simply unlocks advanced features without altering or removing any existing data.</li></ul></div>
<div xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="p"><strong className="ph b">Steps for a smooth transition:</strong><ol className="ol"><li className="li"><strong className="ph b">Backup Your Projects</strong>: Save a backup of your projects locally or in a version control system like Git to protect your work.</li><li className="li"><strong className="ph b">Activate Your License</strong>: Upgrade and activate your paid license. This process won't affect your test cases or project settings.</li><li className="li"><strong className="ph b">Check Feature Compatibility</strong>: Paid versions offer additional features that work seamlessly with your existing projects from the free version.</li></ol></div>
<p xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" className="p">For more details, visit the <a className="xref j-external-link" href="https://docs.katalon.com/" target="_blank">Katalon documentation</a>.</p>
